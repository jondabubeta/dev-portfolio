# Master's Degree: Unlocked

**Published:** June 17, 2026

For a long time, earning a master's degree felt like a distant goal: something I wanted deeply, but could not yet see clearly in front of me. Deciding to attend California State University, Long Beach makes that goal feel real. The door is open, the path is set, and this next chapter has finally been unlocked.

## What This Means to Me

Reaching this point brings together years of work, uncertainty, persistence, and growth. There were times when graduate school felt out of reach, so being able to take this step carries more weight than simply enrolling in another program.

I feel proud of how far I have come, grateful for the people and experiences that helped me get here, and excited by the challenge ahead. There is also some nervousness in beginning something this demanding, but it is the kind of nervousness that comes with knowing I am moving toward something meaningful.

## Why CSULB

At CSULB, I will pursue a master's in Computer Science with a specialization toward artificial intelligence and machine learning. The program gives me an opportunity to deepen the technical foundation I have built through software engineering, data analysis, testing, and applied AI work.

I want to develop stronger research habits, sharpen my systems thinking, and gain a more rigorous understanding of how intelligent systems are designed, evaluated, and improved.

## Looking Ahead

This is not the finish line yet. It is the moment the goal became tangible. I know the work ahead will test me, but I am ready to learn, contribute, and keep growing into the kind of engineer and researcher I want to become.

For now, I am taking a moment to appreciate what this decision represents: a long-held ambition becoming a real next step.
