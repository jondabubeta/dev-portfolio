# Finally did it!

**Published:** June 17, 2026

I have decided to continue my academic path at California State University, Long Beach, where I will pursue a master's in Computer Science with a specialization toward artificial intelligence and machine learning.

## Why CSULB

CSULB gives me a place to deepen the technical foundation I have been building through software engineering, data analysis, and applied AI work. I am choosing this next step because I want stronger research habits, sharper systems thinking, and a more rigorous understanding of how intelligent systems are designed, evaluated, and improved.

## Focus Area

My graduate focus will center on AI and ML, especially the practical questions that sit between model performance and real-world impact:

- How models learn from imperfect data
- How evaluation can reveal reliability and fairness gaps
- How AI systems can be tested, explained, and improved over time
- How machine learning can be used responsibly in software products

## Direction

This decision is also a commitment to the kind of work I want to keep growing into: building useful systems, studying them carefully, and connecting technical implementation with thoughtful evaluation.

I am excited for the next chapter and for the projects, research, and collaborations that will come with it.
